A Florence original

The Newport Steak

Born on Jones Street.

Founder Jack Ubaldi is credited with creating the Newport from the bottom sirloin, a cut most shops ground or sold cheap. Trimmed his way, it gives big beef flavor and real tenderness at a fair price. It has been the house signature ever since.

Thickness
Ask for 1 to 1¼ inches.
Season
Salt generously an hour ahead. Pepper just before cooking.
Cook
Very hot cast iron, 3 to 4 minutes a side for medium rare.
Rest & slice
Rest 5 minutes, then slice across the grain.
