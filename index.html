History

Our Story

Florence opened at 5 Jones Street in 1936. Its remarkable history has repeatedly passed from one butcher who learned the shop to the next.

1936

Jack Ubaldi

An Italian immigrant and master butcher establishes Florence Prime Meat Market at 5 Jones Street.

1970s

Tony Pellegrino

Pellegrino, who had worked at Florence, becomes owner and continues its traditional cut-to-order service.

1990s

Benny Pizzuco

Pizzuco carries Florence through another generation while preserving the shop’s highly personal style of service.

2022

Aristeo Quiñonez

After starting at Florence doing deliveries and cleaning, Quiñonez learns the trade, becomes head butcher and eventually takes over the business with his family.

Four owners. One shop. One tradition.

A Florence Original

The Newport Steak

Born on Jones Street.

Florence is widely credited with originating the Newport Steak, developed by founder Jack Ubaldi from the bottom sirloin. It became a house signature because it delivers flavor, tenderness and value in a compact cut.

Why people love it

  • Rich beef flavor
  • Good tenderness when cooked properly
  • Excellent value
  • A genuine Florence signature
  • Ideal for grilling, cast iron or broiling

Ask for a Newport. We’ll cut it for you.
